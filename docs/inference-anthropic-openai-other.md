## Decision

The 28 missing-usage records in this slice comprise 18 developer/paper/Tinfield records, eight AA records (seven existing full scenarios plus one Fable cost-only proxy), and two Fireworks records. The official Datacurve v1.1 configurations already carry mean USD and mean output tokens; do not duplicate them. For chart prioritization, Datacurve supersedes the five Anthropic Opus 5 developer-effort rows, four AA rows (Opus 5, GPT-5.6 Sol/Luna, GPT-6 Astra), and the two Fireworks rows below. The suppressed records remain source-distinct evidence and in exports. The version-unknown **paper** rows remain separate, including GPT-5.5 xhigh: its 70.0% differs from the v1.1 feed's 67.035%; they are not merged. [Precedence review](official-precedence-review.md).

AA's Coding Agent Index v1.5 averages cost across **DeepSWE v1.1 (113 tasks), Terminal-Bench 4.0 (66), SWE-Atlas-QnA (124)**, with no per-benchmark cost allocation; its `Tokens / Task` combines input, cached input, cache writes, reasoning and output. The **separate Intelligence Index** explicitly reports output-only/task: GPT-6 Sol ~31k, Luna ~51k; GPT-5.6 Sol ~29k, Luna ~41k; Opus 5 ~73k, Opus 5.5 ~119k; Fable 5.1 max with fallback ~78k. Existing OpenAI output method is `target Intelligence output × mean(60,013.644/29,000, 73,399.708/41,000)`; its cost fit uses three same-model/max AA-Coding/USD versus Datacurve/USD pairs, `slope = Σ(AA_cost × DeepSWE_cost)/Σ(AA_cost²) = 1.1482398469`, multiplied by AA's $2.99/$0.18 suite means. The Opus 5.5 one-pair output and cost formulas are `119,000 × (117,565.694/73,000)` and `$13.04 × ($11.837583/$10.79)`. The existing [research record](../data/research/usage-proxy-cross-platform-20260927.json) preserves full-precision operands, attempted counts and sensitivity/leave-one-out calculations. Direct Datacurve means on the four superseded AA model/max records are cross-harness references only, not replacement measurements for their runs.

Fable's research-only sensitivity uses exact [Datacurve Fable **5**, max](https://deepswe.datacurve.ai/artifacts/v1.1/leaderboard-live.json) 118,592.77293577982 mean output tokens over 436 scored attempts, multiplied separately by [Firecrawl's](https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/is-fable-5-1-cheaper-than-fable-5) same-max Fable 5.1/Fable 5 output totals `10,026/7,727` (three prompts **without tools**, not DeepSWE), or [AA's](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/claude-fable-5-1) **approximately** 1.7× ratio on its Intelligence Index. [AA's September Astra article](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/benchmarking-gpt-6-astra) independently gives ~78k Intelligence Index output tokens/task for Fable 5.1 with fallback; **78k is not a DeepSWE count**. AA says fallback served ~4% of its Intelligence Index output tokens, while neither the Anthropic developer report nor Datacurve Fable 5 states matching fallback routing. The two incompatible workloads give ~154k versus ~202k; do not average them or label them a CI. The 57 Firecrawl API-billed runs are unrelated coding/prompt tasks, not benchmark observations. There is no DeepSWE-matched input/cache-write/read mix for repricing output into a total. The [Anthropic price list](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing) gives Fable 5.1 $10/M input, $50/M output, $0.25/M cache hits, and different 5m/1h cache-write rates; even the output-only component for these hypotheses does **not** establish a full task bill.

## Rows without a defensible additional estimate

- `anthropic-claude-opus-5.5-v1.1` (74.2% average over five trials) and `anthropic-claude-fable-5.1-v1.1` (67.4% average over five trials): **effort and harness not specified** in the cited system-card result. Do not substitute AA max or Fable 5 max. They have no source-reported per-task usage/cost.
- Five Anthropic Opus 5 developer effort rows are chart-superseded by Datacurve's same-model/effort v1.1 usage. Keep exact source results separate; do not copy feed means into the developer experiment. AA Opus 5 max, AA GPT-5.6 Sol/Luna max and AA GPT-6 Astra max likewise already have existing cross-harness scenarios but their chart rows are superseded. Fireworks Opus 5 max **$11.838/task** and GPT-6 Astra xhigh **$6.524/task** remain source-scoped only; their version, harness, denominator and accounting basis are unspecified, and their chart marks are suppressed.
- `badtheorylabs-tinfield-1-v1.1`: the [model card](https://huggingface.co/badtheorylabs/Tinfield-1) states mini-swe-agent, full 113 tasks, k=5 and raw score 62, but gives no output-token counts, DeepSWE task costs, provider bill/rate or comparable effort. Its 6.6B active parameters/64GB-machine claim does not give tokens or dollars. Do not equate its Qwen-derived weights or comparison rows to another model's usage.
- Eight Datacurve original-paper rows in this slice (GPT-5.5 xhigh; GPT-5.4 xhigh/mini xhigh; Claude Opus 4.6/4.7 max, Sonnet 4.6 high, Haiku 4.5 default; MiniMax M2.7 default) have version **unspecified**. [Figure 8](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.07946v1) plots **median** output tokens and median USD per *trial* but prints no per-model numeric values; these cannot be inferred from positions or equated with a different v1.1 mean. Default effort is not high/max. No cost or output scenario is justified for those paper observations.

List prices corroborate *rates*, not benchmark totals: [Anthropic's rate card](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing) gives Opus 5.5 $4/$20 per million input/output with $0.20/M cache hits, Fable 5.1 $10/$50; [AA's OpenAI release analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier) gives Sol $2/$10 and Luna $0.10/$0.50 with cache discounts/write premiums. Anthropic's [worked Claude Code example](https://claude.com/blog/what-a-task-costs-on-opus-5-5) calls its token totals *illustrations*, not DeepSWE telemetry. [Together's independent DeepSWE Fable **5** comparison](https://www.together.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-pro-0813-vs-claude-fable-5-on-deepswe-cost-coding-and-routing) reproduces a ~115k output / $21.63 Fable **5** max run, **not Fable 5.1**; it cannot be transferred as a Fable 5.1 bill. The primary OpenAI announcement was retrieved and hashed 2026-09-26 in the existing source capture but returned HTTP 403 on this research pass; no new OpenAI price/usage fact is attributed to a fresh fetch.

**Next useful evidence:** Per-target model+effort+fallback/harness DeepSWE output counts, and billed fresh-input/cache-hit/cache-write/output counts with pricing snapshot, would support better Fable 5.1, OpenAI developer and Tinfield cost estimates. Until then the source-specific gaps above remain null; none of these research-only sensitivity cases is suitable as a measured data point or statistical interval.
